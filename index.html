For you, Zhane. With love, from Kxn_eth. 🎀

Happy 18th birthday, Zhane! 🥹❤️


Grabe, 18 ka na. Parang kailan lang nung Grade 12, 15 ka palang. Hindi ko rin inexpect na sa simpleng pagiging magkaklase natin, magiging ganito tayo ka-close. Thank you kasi ever since, naging mabait ka sa akin/samin. Sobrang genuine mo rin as a person, and siguro yun yung isa sa mga dahilan kung bakit naging comfortable kami sa'yo.


Kahit sobrang kulit mo minsan HAHAHA, at sobrang soft-hearted mo, tinuring ka na talaga namin na parang babaeng kapatid. Yung tipong aasarin ka namin, kukulitin ka, pero at the same time, gusto rin namin lagi kang okay.


Hindi man ako palaging vocal about these things, pero I'm really thankful na nakilala kita. Thank you for all the memories, sa mga tawanan, sa mga random na kwentuhan, at kahit sa mga simpleng moments na baka hindi natin napapansin ngayon pero eventually magiging memories na lang natin.


Ngayong 18 ka na, don't be afraid to grow and become the person you want to be. Marami ka pang pagdadaanan, marami ka pang matututunan, and I'm sure may mga times na mapapagod ka or madidiscourage ka. Pero sana kahit ganun, piliin mo pa rin bumangon at ipagpatuloy yung mga bagay na mahalaga sa'yo.


You're only 18, and it's okay if you're still figuring things out. Hindi mo kailangang ikumpara yung sarili mo sa ibang tao. Your journey is your own, and darating din yung time na makikita mo kung bakit kailangan mong pagdaanan yung mga bagay na pinagdadaanan mo ngayon.


And since you're such a soft-hearted person, sana matutunan mo rin na hindi lahat ng tao deserve yung kindness and energy na binibigay mo. Continue being kind, pero don't forget to protect yourself too.


You deserve people who will appreciate you, respect you, and make you feel valued. I'm really proud of the person you're becoming, Zhane. From the girl I met in Grade 12, ngayon you're already entering a new chapter of your life.


Ang dami mo pang mararanasan, ang dami mo pang dreams na matutupad, and I hope you never stop believing in yourself. You deserve happiness, success, genuine friendships, and people who will stay even when things aren't easy.


No matter where life takes us, sana hindi mawala yung pagiging close natin. Kahit hindi na tayo magkaklase tulad dati, you'll always be one of those people I'll be grateful that I met.


Happy 18th birthday, Zhane. ❤️

Stay kind, stay genuine, and stay the soft-hearted person that we know and love.

Enjoy your day. You deserve to be celebrated. 🥹❤️
